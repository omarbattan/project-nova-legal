💡 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do the starting gun audio cues work?

Sprint Starter delivers official athletics cadence: 'On your marks', 'Set', followed by the start signal. You can randomize the 'Set to Go' delay to simulate authentic race conditions and prevent anticipating the gun.

Q: Can I use external Bluetooth speakers or headphones?

Yes. Sprint Starter routes audio through any connected device. Ensure your iPhone ringer is unmuted and the volume is turned up for the best experience on the track.

Q: How do I restore my PRO purchase?

Tap the Settings icon inside Sprint Starter and select 'Restore Purchases'.
