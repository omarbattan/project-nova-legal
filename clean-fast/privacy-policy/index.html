Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the CleanFast mobile application ("Application"), developed and operated by Omar Battan ("Service Provider", "we", "us").

1. Zero Data Collection Principle

CleanFast is designed with a privacy-first, anti-tracking philosophy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, fasting logs, health data, or analytics to external servers.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use CleanFast.
  • Local Storage Only: All your fasting sessions, streak records, protocol preferences, and streak freezes are saved exclusively on your device using local storage (SharedPreferences).
  • No External Tracking or Third-Party Analytics: CleanFast does not embed Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, Firebase Analytics, or any advertising SDKs.

2. Device Permissions

CleanFast may request the following device permission solely to provide core offline functionality:

  • Notifications: Used strictly on-device via local notifications to remind you when your fasting window starts, reaches the halfway mark, and completes. Notification scheduling occurs entirely on your device with no remote servers involved.

3. In-App Purchases (StoreKit / Google Play)

Purchases for optional Lifetime PRO (CleanFast PRO) and Consumable Streak Freezes are processed directly and securely by Apple (StoreKit) or Google (Play Billing). We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, deleting the application permanently removes all your fasting history, streak records, and local settings immediately.

5. Children's Privacy

CleanFast is not directed to individuals under the age of 16. Fasting routines are not recommended for children or adolescents. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors.

6. Health & Medical Disclaimer

CleanFast is a habit-tracking and productivity tool for general wellness and educational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide clinical diagnoses or individualized medical guidance. Consult a qualified medical practitioner before undertaking any intermittent fasting regimen.
